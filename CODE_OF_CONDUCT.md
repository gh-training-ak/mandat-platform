# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their experience level, background or identity.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being kind when someone is wrong, including when it is you
- Giving feedback on the code, not the person
- Assuming the other person had a reason

Things that do not:

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissive comments
- Publishing someone's private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports are
handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
